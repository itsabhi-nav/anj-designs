# Open questions for the business

Nothing on the live site was silently rewritten. The items below are published
somewhere in the company's own material but are inconsistent or undefined.
The site currently shows the **more conservative** reading and flags the
discrepancy in the copy where it matters.

Please confirm each one so the final wording can be locked.

---

## 1. Company history — which dates are correct?

| Source | Says |
| --- | --- |
| `about-us` page | "Back in 2010, Annie Nagpal Jain turned a simple idea into a design journey…" |
| Portfolio PDF, *About Us* page | "Decor Inn gallery was started by ANNIE NAGPAL JAIN in 2010" |
| Portfolio PDF, *About Annie Jain* | "In **2017** she established her own venture DÉCOR INN" |
| Home page, founder biography | "In **2016** she established her own venture DECOR INN now ventured into ANJ DESIGNS BY DECOR INN" |
| Home page, founder biography | "…since **2006**" |

**Currently on the site:** the About page timeline shows `2006`, `2010` and a
`2016 – 2017` band with an explicit note that the two sources disagree. The
home page keeps the original "2016" sentence verbatim.

**Needed:** confirmation of the year Décor Inn was established as Annie's own
venture, and whether "Decor Inn gallery" (2010) and the "venture" are the same
milestone or two.

## 4. Scope of architectural design

Every page of the original site states: *"We provides a full range of interior
design, architectural design."* No service page defines what that covers.

**Currently on the site:** Architectural Design is presented as a fourth
offering with neutral wording and the capability list limited to the project
types already named in the founder biography (houses, restaurants, cafés,
schools, banks, hotels, offices). A note on the services page states that the
scope is unconfirmed.

**Needed:** whether the studio offers full architectural services (planning
drawings, approvals, structural co-ordination) or interior-led architectural
support only.

---

## 5. Scope of turnkey execution

"Turnkey Interior Design" is listed as a service but the deliverables are never
enumerated.

**Currently on the site:** the turnkey copy describes the studio's own
process — design, in-house production of furniture and wall panels, and
delivery — without promising civil work, electrical, plumbing, procurement,
installation or project management.

**Needed:** the exact inclusions, so the wording can be made specific. Any
inclusion that is added must be added to the capability list as well.

---

## 6. Project descriptions

The 13 projects are published on the original site with **a name, a
photograph and nothing else** — no description, area, budget, client,
materials or completion date.

**Currently on the site:** every project page states plainly that the studio
has not released those details, and presents the work image-led. The short
"note" on each card is an editorial description of what the photograph shows,
not a claim about the project.

**Needed:** if the studio would like real copy, area, scope or a client
credit on any project, it needs to be supplied. Nothing has been invented.
